## 今日頭條

**Anthropic 6/27 公開〈Statement on the US government directive to suspend access to Fable 5 and Mythos 5〉——frontier AI 廠商第一次對「政府安全 claim」公開反擊，NSA red-team test 細節成焦點** —— [Anthropic 官方聲明](https://www.anthropic.com/news/fable-mythos-access)（6/27 公開）。**這是 2026 年中 frontier AI 與 US 政府之間最戲劇化的對抗升級**，重要性超過 [6/25 Anthropic Fable 5 + Mythos 5 launch](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-fable-5-mythos-5) 與 [6/24 Anthropic v Alibaba letter](https://github.com/Aiden128/ai-daily-digest/daily/2026-06-26) 的蒸餾指控。**核心事件**：(1) **6/27 5:21pm ET，US government 透過「national security authorities」向 Anthropic 發出 export control directive**——要求 Anthropic **abruptly disable Fable 5 + Mythos 5 給所有 customer**（無論 foreign national 在 US 內外，包括 foreign national Anthropic employees）、**其他 model 不受影響**；(2) **Anthropic 公開的「**反擊聲明**」**：「**The letter did not provide specific details of its national security concern**」、「**Our understanding is that the government believes it has become aware of a method of bypassing, or "jailbreaking" Fable 5**」、「**We have not even received a disclosure of a concerning non-universal potential jailbreak that led to a harmful result.
